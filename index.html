About

Built for the culture, designed for precision

Fresh Look Barbershop is more than a place to get a haircut. It's where craftsmanship meets community, a space where every barber takes pride in sending you out looking and feeling your absolute best.

Located on Rue Rideau in the heart of Ottawa, we specialize in fades, lineups, beard shaping, and textured cuts for all hair types.
